**Subject:** Fixing the shade mismatch when you re-run a tile — proposal in one page

**What we understood.** When you re-run a tile, the printed shade drifts from the master, and your team spends 6–7 hours on trial-and-error rounds while thousands of square metres come out the wrong shade. Your lab can already *measure* the difference. What nobody can tell them is **how much to change the printer file**.

**What we propose.** A software tool called Tessera. Your team measures the new tile as they do today. Tessera works out the change needed for each ink and produces a corrected print file. A QC supervisor approves it before anything goes to the printer. It also keeps a permanent record per tile design, always compared with the original master, so old "drifted" references stop accumulating errors. It runs on a PC at the plant, needs no connection to the printer or kiln, and does not run continuously on the line, because your problem happens at batch change.

**One honest point.** The tile still has to be fired, and firing takes time. The calculation takes minutes. We expect your team to reach an approved file in **about 1.5 hours instead of 6–7**, with one test print instead of many. We will measure this during the pilot, not assume it.
